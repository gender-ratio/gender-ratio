Gender Ratio in Schooling

Equal Classrooms, Stronger Communities

A Community Service Project dedicated to tracking student enrollment, attendance, transfer, and dropout patterns across local schools—helping identify disparities and ensure equal educational opportunities for every learner.

Why Gender Ratio Matters

Monitoring the balance of girls and boys in classrooms goes beyond numbers—it reveals how accessible, safe, and supportive our education system truly is.

1. Early Detection of Dropouts

Tracking year-over-year gender ratios highlights when and where students—especially girls—leave school early due to financial, social, or transport barriers.

2. Equal Access & Representation

A balanced classroom ensures every child receives equal attention, mentorship, and confidence to pursue higher studies and STEM or leadership careers.

3. Targeted Scholarships & Support

Accurate school-level data helps communities, teachers, and policymakers direct scholarships, uniforms, and counseling to the schools that need them most.

4. Stronger Economic & Social Growth

Educating all genders equally leads to healthier families, higher household incomes, and stronger long-term development across the entire community.
