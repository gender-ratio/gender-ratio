Team Summary

Our team of four worked together to study school transportation and present our findings in different formats. We visited schools, collected data, and conducted surveys to understand how students travel to school every day.

Deepika created this website, Baby prepared the project document, and Thaneesha designed the presentation. Sreeja, along with the entire team, contributed to data collection and surveys. Together, we combined our efforts to complete this project successfully.

We are a team of 4 students. For our Community Service Project, we worked together on survey, awareness and field work in BV Nagar and Padarupalli, Nellore.
